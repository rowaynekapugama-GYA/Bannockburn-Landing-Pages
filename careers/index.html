Who We Are

For more than 30 years, Bannockburn Dental has proudly supported our community with dental care that's grounded in compassion, quality and genuine relationships, built on our family values over two generations.

Located just out of Geelong, we have grown substantially with our community and maintained the country-town feel. We are always keen to hear from people who share our values and want to be part of a friendly, inclusive and supportive team.

We believe every role in our practice is vital, and that includes yours. Our team thrives on respect, trust and a shared sense of purpose.

What We Offer

Whether you're just starting out or an experienced clinician, you'll have real support behind you.

New to dental assisting? You'll learn on the job with one-on-one mentoring from a dedicated team member, following a clear training plan so you always know what's next. When you're ready, we'll support you to complete your Cert III in Dental Assisting through TAFE.

An experienced clinician? You'll join a modern practice with CBCT, OPG and same-day CEREC crowns, a loyal local patient base, and an experienced team that lets you focus on great dentistry. We work as a collaborative practice with active mentoring and support.

  • Above-award pay rates
  • One-on-one mentoring and a structured training program
  • Support to complete your Cert III in Dental Assisting
  • Modern technology and a well-equipped practice
  • Staff dental discount (T&Cs apply)
  • A friendly team with social events throughout the year

Roles We Hire For

Dental Assistant

Chairside support across general, restorative and preventive care, plus sterilisation, room preparation and helping patients feel at ease.

Reception & Front Desk

The first friendly face our patients meet, looking after bookings, enquiries and the smooth running of each day.

Dentist

Excel in general and complex dental treatment with the support of mentorship and modern technology. Experienced and new graduates welcome.

Oral Health Therapist

Prevention, hygiene and children's care, working closely with our dentists to support patients' long-term oral health.
