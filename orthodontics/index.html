Is aligner therapy right for you?

What clear aligners can correct

Clear aligners work best for mild to moderate alignment issues — we'll assess your smile and let you know if it's the right choice for you.

Crowded teethGently create space and bring teeth into line.
Gaps and spacingClose spaces for a fuller, even smile.
Mild bite issuesImprove how your teeth meet and function.
Removable comfortTake them out for meals, brushing and special occasions.
Easier hygieneStraight teeth — and removable trays — are easier to keep clean.
Fewer visitsFewer appointments than traditional fixed braces.

Your aligner journey

See your new smile before you begin

Consultation & assessment

Digital smile preview

Custom fabrication

Treatment progression

Retainers & results

After your final set, retainers maintain your new smile and prevent relapse.

Kids & teens

Orthodontics for growing smiles

As a family practice, we monitor your child's tooth and jaw development at their regular check-ups — so if alignment issues are developing, we catch them early and plan the right treatment at the right time.

  • Early orthodontic assessments — we keep an eye on crowding, spacing and bite development from a young age, and advise on the ideal timing for treatment.
  • Discreet options for teens — clear aligners are nearly invisible and removable, a popular choice for self-conscious teenagers.
  • A team kids actually like — we love seeing children and children love seeing us; Dr Sapana and Dr Meenakshi are especially great at putting young patients at ease.
  • Straighter teeth, healthier habits — well-aligned teeth are easier to clean, setting kids up for a lifetime of good oral health.
