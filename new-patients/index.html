Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer services for nervous patients?

Yes! We provide happy gas (nitrous oxide) and a calming environment to help ease dental anxiety.

What age should my child first visit the dentist?

We recommend starting check-ups around age 3. Introductory visits from age 2 during a parent's appointment are also a great idea.

Do you bulk bill for kids?

Yes — if your child is eligible under Medicare's Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS), we can bulk bill their treatment.

What are your fees like?

We aim to keep our fees below average and offer transparent pricing with no hidden costs.

Do you accept emergency appointments?

Absolutely. We keep space available each day for dental emergencies — call us ASAP and we'll do our best to help.
