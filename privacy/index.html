Privacy policy

Your privacy at Bannockburn Dental

Bannockburn Dental respects your privacy and handles personal and health information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect. When you book online or contact us, we collect the details needed to arrange your care, such as your name, phone number, email and appointment preferences. Clinical information is collected only as part of your dental treatment.

How we use it. We use your information to book and confirm appointments, provide treatment, send reminders and respond to your enquiries. We do not sell your personal information.

Online bookings. Appointments booked online are handled through our secure practice booking system.

Website analytics and advertising. This website uses cookies and tools such as Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and advertising platforms to understand how visitors use the site and measure our advertising. These tools do not collect your health information. You can manage or block cookies in your browser settings.

Access and questions. You can ask to access or correct the information we hold about you, or raise a privacy concern, by contacting the practice on (03) 4829 3917 or visiting us at 4/13 High Street, Bannockburn VIC 3331.
